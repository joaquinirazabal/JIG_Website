Research and transfer

Projects

A selection of the research and technology-transfer projects I have contributed to since 2011, covering dam safety, numerical modelling, machine learning and industrial applications.

DAMSHAI

2025–2028 · Dam safety

Dam Structural Health Monitoring and Safety Assessment with an AI Agent

Development of AI agents for dam safety that integrate monitoring data, numerical models and expert knowledge to support structural health monitoring and decision-making.

OPTIFLOW

2025–2028 · Industrial Engineering

Intelligent Optimisation of Preforms and Parameters in Flowforming

Intelligent optimisation of preforms and process parameters in flowforming, combining advanced simulation and artificial intelligence.

EASY-HYDRO

2025–2027 · Environmental Engineering

CFD Software for Dams and Reversible Hydropower Plants

Software development for hydrodynamic modelling and optimisation of dams and reversible hydropower plants.

RESILINK

2024–2026 · Industrial Engineering · Team member

Resilient Buildings Equipped with Shear-Link Dissipators

Numerical modelling and optimisation of shear-link energy dissipation devices for more resilient buildings.

DOLMEN

2022–2025 · Dams · Team member

Dam Safety Control with Dynamic Warning Thresholds

Hybrid methodology combining numerical models and machine learning to predict dam behaviour and establish dynamic warning thresholds.

OPTIPRO

2020–2023 · Industrial Engineering · Team member

Intelligent Optimisation of Rotary Metal Forming Processes

Advanced numerical simulation and machine learning for the optimisation of cylindrical rolling and flowforming processes.

TRISTAN

2019–2021 · Dams · Co-Principal Investigator

New Computational Tools for Reliability-Based Dam Safety Assessment

Computational methodologies for dam safety assessment combining reliability analysis, numerical simulation and data-driven techniques.

NUMA

2016–2018 · Dams · Code development coordinator

Platform for Physics-Based and Data-Driven Dam Monitoring

Platform integrating physics-based numerical models and data-driven models into dam monitoring management.
