About me

Machine learning and numerical simulation for engineering

I am an Assistant Research Professor in the Machine Learning in Civil Engineering group at CIMNE, where I have worked since 2011. My research connects computational mechanics with data-driven methods to optimise engineering processes and support the safety of civil infrastructure.

Trained as an industrial engineer, I completed a PhD in Structural Analysis at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya on the discrete element modelling of railway ballast, which received the XVI Talgo Award for Technological Innovation. Since 2019 my work has focused on applying machine learning to industrial engineering and dam safety: surrogate models for process optimisation, automatic calibration of numerical models, and hybrid approaches for monitoring and anomaly detection. Granular materials and railway infrastructure were the focus of my earlier research and remain part of my technical background.

What I work on

Research areas

My work combines physics-based simulation, data and machine learning, with industrial engineering and dam safety as the main application areas.

01

Machine learning for industrial engineering

Surrogate models and simulation-based optimisation for flowforming, metal forming and resilient structural devices.

02

Dam safety and monitoring

Data-driven and hybrid models for interpreting monitoring data, detecting anomalies and supporting dam safety assessment.

03

Numerical modelling and scientific software

Finite element and particle methods, model calibration and research software, including earlier work on granular materials and railway infrastructure.
