About me

Numerical modelling and machine learning for civil engineering

I am an Assistant Research Professor in the Machine Learning in Civil Engineering group at CIMNE, where I have worked since 2011. My research connects computational mechanics with data-driven methods to model, monitor and understand the behaviour of civil engineering structures and materials.

Trained as an industrial engineer, I completed a PhD in Structural Analysis at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya on the discrete element modelling of railway ballast, which received the XVI Talgo Award for Technological Innovation. Since 2019 my work has focused increasingly on data-driven and hybrid methods for dam safety, and on using machine learning to calibrate and interpret numerical models.

What I work on

Research areas

My work combines physics-based simulation, monitoring data and machine learning in three connected areas.

01

Dam safety and monitoring

Data-driven and hybrid models for interpreting monitoring data, detecting anomalies and supporting dam safety assessment.

02

Granular materials and railway infrastructure

Discrete element modelling of ballast and granular media, particle shape and contact, DEM–FEM coupling and rockfall protection.

03

Machine learning for engineering models

Surrogate models, automatic calibration of numerical parameters and interpretation of machine learning predictions.
